NoiseModelling days 2025

Dear NoiseModelling Community,

We are thrilled to announce the organization of the first "in person" NoiseModelling Days 2025.

🗓️ June 26-27, 2025 - Immediately following the Forum Acusticum 2025 (https://www.fa-euronoise2025.org/)

🌍 Malaga, Spain 🇪🇸

🏫 Sala de Grados A, School of Telecommunication Engineering, Teatinos Campus (map link)

While we are excited to finally meet many of you face-to-face, we will also provide a hybrid connection to ensure those unable to travel can still participate.

Organizational details are still being finalized, but we hope and anticipate that the seminar will be free for all participants. More information will be shared in early 2025, so stay tuned!

In the meantime, we encourage you to attend the Forum Acusticum 2025 and save your Thursday and Friday for the NoiseModelling Days 2025.
