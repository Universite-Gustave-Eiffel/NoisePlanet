Training course

Objective

The NoiseModelling team plans to organise training sessions to introduce participants to the field of environmental acoustics and provide practical experience in using software for modelling and mapping noise (from road and rail traffic).

Next session

🗓️ When?

March 11-12, 2027

🌍 Where?

At the Gustave Eiffel University campus in Nantes

🪙 How much?

The fee is €1,000 for the two-day session

📚 Program

The training session, which is scheduled to take place over two days, consists of four modules:

Day 1

- Environmental acoustics: background, regulations, etc.
- Input data handling: theory and practice

Day 2

- Getting started with NoiseModelling
- Advanced use of the tool

For whom?

This training session is mainly designed for public bodies or engineering consultancies, but everybody is welcomed!

Who will receive the funds collected?

- Approximately 80% of the total amount will go to the NoiseModelling project. This money will be hosted by Gustave Eiffel University but will used for the all community.
- The remaining 20% covers training administration costs and the use of the facilities.

Who will receive the funds collected?

The money raised will be used, in priority, for:
- organising future NoiseModelling Days
- organising future Coding Camps
- present NoiseModelling (the tool, use cases, results, ...) in conferences
- engaging with the community

In which language? 🇫🇷 🇬🇧

It will depend on the participants.
By default, we will offer training courses in French.
However, if enough people request a course in English (or even Spanish), this could be considered.
